When are you coming

There is three most beautiful seasons to visit Bangladesh. Choose the one suits you.

summer:This is a beautiful season in Bangladesh specially for beach (Cox's bazar, Saint martin). Also it offers long day light and fresh seasonal fruits.

it is usually sunny , Hot and Humid.
The temperature is usually 30°C – 41°C.

monsoon:This is a great season to see lush green landscapes and full flowing- rivers specially in swamp forests(Ratargul, Tanguar Haor, Sylhet). Also you can get cheaper hotel rates and avoid heavy tourist crowds.

it is usually rainy , overcast and humid.
The temperature is usually 28°C – 32°C.

winter:This is the most comfortable season for traveling around Bangladesh specially for forest tours and hiking (Sundarbans, Bandarban). Also you can enjoy traditional winter sweets and see thousands of migratory birds.

it is usually cool , dry and sunny.
The temperature is usually 10°C – 22°C.
